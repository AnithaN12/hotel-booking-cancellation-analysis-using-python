# 📌 Business Insights

* High Cancellation Rate: 37.13% of bookings were canceled, indicating significant revenue leakage and booking instability.
* City Hotel Vulnerability: City Hotels recorded a 41.71% cancellation rate compared to 27.97% for Resort Hotels, highlighting the need for stricter booking policies.
* Seasonal Demand: July and August experienced the highest booking volumes and cancellations, requiring better peak-season inventory planning.
* Pricing Impact: Canceled bookings showed higher average ADR than completed bookings, suggesting a potential relationship between premium pricing and cancellation behaviour.
* Geographical Risk: Portugal accounted for 70.08% of cancellations in the reported country analysis, making it an important segment for further investigation.
* Market Segment Risk: Online Travel Agents (Online TA) and group bookings contributed substantially to cancellations, highlighting opportunities for improved channel and group booking management.
* Revenue Optimization: Targeted deposits, non-refundable rate plans, and flexible pricing strategies can help improve booking commitment.
  
# 🚀 Strategic Business Recommendations
* Reduce Cancellations: Introduce advance deposits and non-refundable booking options.
* Optimize Inventory: Develop predictive overbooking models, particularly for City Hotels.
* Improve Seasonal Planning: Adjust pricing and staffing based on monthly demand patterns.
*Target High-Risk Segments: Develop customized cancellation policies based on booking channels and customer segments.
* Recover Lost Revenue: Launch targeted marketing campaigns to convert canceled bookings into new reservations.
* Strengthen Forecasting: Use historical cancellation patterns to improve occupancy and revenue forecasts.
  
# 🎯 Business Impact

The analysis provides actionable insights to help hotel management:

* Minimize revenue leakage.

* Optimize pricing strategies.
* Reduce booking uncertainty.
* Strengthen revenue management decisions.
